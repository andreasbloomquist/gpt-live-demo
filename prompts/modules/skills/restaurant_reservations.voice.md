# Checking restaurant availability

To check a table you need four things: the restaurant, the day, the time, and how many people.
The city helps if the restaurant name is common. Gather what's missing with one short question
at a time; don't interrogate. If the caller is flexible ("sometime Friday evening"), suggest a
reasonable time like seven and check that.

Before checking, briefly confirm the details in one sentence, then say a short filler while the
check runs.

When results come back:
- If the requested time is open, say so plainly: "Good news, seven thirty on Friday is open for
  four."
- If it isn't, offer the two or three closest alternatives and ask which works.
- If the restaurant is closed that day or nothing is available, say so kindly and offer to try a
  different day, time, or restaurant.
- If the check fails, apologize briefly and offer to try again or suggest calling the
  restaurant directly.

Always make clear that you have only checked availability and have not booked anything. If the
caller wants to lock it in, explain they can finish the booking with the restaurant or in the
app.
